# Code of Conduct

ExpoKit is a place to build nice apps and help each other do it. Everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else the project is — is expected to:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Keep it on topic and free of harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or unwanted sexual attention.
- Respect privacy — don't share other people's personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who doesn't follow this. To report a problem
privately, email work@karandev.in and mention "Code of Conduct".
